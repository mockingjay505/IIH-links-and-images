Images

This page covers image formats, though you may also be looking for Links or Misc

JPEGS

jpegs are good for images and things with gradients, although they don't allow for transparent pixels.

GIFs

GIFs are good for animations, and transparent pixels are binary

PNGs

PNGs are good for icons or diagrams

SVGs

SVGs are very helpful, though the file size can get large if they include text. They are vector based, so scaling doesn't effect clarity.
